## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Deliria**. This trainer enhances Deliria's sandbox and action-RPG systems with optional quality-of-life controls for health, mana, stamina, resources, combat power, movement, and building. It is designed for private sessions, creative experimentation, exploration, and testing different weapons, spells, mounts, pets, and summons.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Deliria's infinite sandbox, experiment with terrain sculpting and construction, test weapons and abilities, practice against bosses, or reduce resource and survival pressure during private play.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the player's health from decreasing during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Mana** — Keeps the mana pool full for repeated spell casting and summon use.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Allows extended movement and combat actions without exhausting the stamina resource.
- **Infinite Resources** — Provides unlimited usable building and crafting resources where supported by the current game build.
- **Easy Crafting** — Removes or reduces material requirements for crafting and construction.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Style Rank Protection** — Helps preserve the combat style rank while fighting with swords, guns, bows, magic, and bombs.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Greatly increases outgoing damage against ordinary enemies and bosses.
- **No Damage** — Prevents incoming damage during invasions, Blood Moons, and boss encounters.
- **Unlimited Summons** — Allows continued use of pets and summoned allies without normal resource limitations.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Temporarily stops nearby enemies to make encounters easier to study or manage.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Enhancements** — Improves movement freedom for traversal across caves, ramps, overhangs, and other sculpted terrain.
- **Fast Travel Movement** — Provides faster traversal when exploring biomes, towns, arenas, and underground areas.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options to travel across Deliria's smooth terrain, caves, ramps, arenas, and overhangs more freely. Movement enhancements are especially useful when testing mounts, wings, grappling traversal, or large custom-built areas. Disable them before entering competitive multiplayer.

### Tools & Inventory

Resource and crafting options are intended for building experiments and sandbox testing. Activate them before collecting or crafting, then use the normal inventory and crafting interfaces. Avoid changing large quantities of items during world-saving operations and keep a backup of important saves.

### World & Environment

Deliria allows players to sculpt terrain, create forests, caverns, arenas, towns, and cities without a cube-based grid. The trainer is useful for testing construction projects, exploring the game's biomes, reaching difficult areas, and examining locations connected to invasions, missions, and the underground dimension.

### Combat & Weapons

Deliria supports swords, guns, bows, magic, bombs, pets, summons, mounts, and style-based combat scoring. Use defensive options for boss study or Blood Moon testing, and use damage options to shorten encounters. Style Rank Protection helps maintain combat momentum while experimenting with different weapon types.
